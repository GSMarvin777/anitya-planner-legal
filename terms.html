What this app is

Anitya Planner Analytics ("the app") is an internal tool built by the Anitya team. It connects to Anitya's own social media accounts, including our TikTok account, and reads public statistics about our own posts so our team can track how our content performs.

Who may use it

The app is only for members of the Anitya team. It is not available to the public, and no one outside the team is asked to connect an account to it.

What the app does and does not do

  • It reads basic profile information and public statistics (such as views, likes, comments and shares) for videos posted by the accounts the Anitya team has connected.
  • It does not post, edit, delete or download any content.
  • It does not read private messages or information about other TikTok users.

Platform rules

Use of TikTok data through the app follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and TikTok's platform policies. Access can be removed at any time by revoking the app's permission in the connected TikTok account's settings.

No warranty

The app is provided as is for internal use. Statistics may be delayed or incomplete, depending on what each platform makes available.
