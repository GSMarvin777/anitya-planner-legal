Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Whose data

Anitya Planner Analytics is an internal tool of the Anitya team. It only accesses data from social media accounts that belong to Anitya and that our team has connected on purpose, such as our TikTok account.

What we collect

  • Basic profile information of our own connected account, such as its username and follower count.
  • Public statistics for videos posted by that account, such as views, likes, comments, shares and video length.

We do not collect private messages, contact lists, or any information about people who watch or comment on our videos.

How we use it

The statistics are shown in our team's internal content planner so we can see which posts perform well and plan future content. They are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are kept on a team member's computer. The statistics are stored in the team's internal planner, which only Anitya team members can open.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep the statistics for as long as they help us plan content. The connected account can revoke the app's access at any time in its TikTok settings; after that the app can no longer read any data. To ask us to delete stored statistics, email us at the address below.
