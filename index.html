Anitya Planner Analytics

An internal tool of the Anitya team

Anitya Planner Analytics reads the public statistics of Anitya's own social media accounts, such as views, likes, comments and shares on our videos, and shows them in the content planner our team uses. It does not post, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to the public.
